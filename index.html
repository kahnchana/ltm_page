LangToMo

A hierarchical vision–language–action framework for robot control.

About

LangToMo combines a diffusion-based motion generator (System 2) with an action mapping module (System 1). It learns to produce interpretable optical-flow representations from language instructions, then translates those flows into real robot actions.

Features

  • Optical flow as a universal motion representation
  • Text‑conditioned diffusion model for motion generation
  • Learned & hand‑crafted mappings to robot actions
  • Zero‑shot and low‑supervision capabilities
